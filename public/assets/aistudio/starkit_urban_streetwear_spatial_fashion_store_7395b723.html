01. What is the fabric specification? +

One-side brushed fleece, combed compact cotton (330–350 GSM for Drop 01 Hoodies; 280 GSM for Core Tees; 480 GSM for Heavy Loopback).

Available in M (40"×27.5"), L (42"×28.5"), XL (44"×29.5"), and XXL (46"×30.5"). Exact chest and length inch measurements are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before dispatch.

Order 2 pieces to automatically save BDT 150 + Free Delivery, or 3+ pieces to save BDT 300 + Free Delivery. Checkout via our web bag or forward your SKU + Color + Size to WhatsApp +880 1931-803462.

04. What about nationwide delivery & 48-hour exchanges? +

Nationwide courier delivery across Bangladesh via Pathao & RedX (Inside Dhaka: BDT 70, Outside Dhaka: BDT 130; FREE on 2+ pieces). Cash on Delivery, bKash, and Nagad supported with a 48-hour size or defect exchange guarantee.
